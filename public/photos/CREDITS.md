# Photo credits

The sample grid uses free photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) (and, where a download failed, [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos)) so the demo looks like a camera roll. They are not the user's private photos, and they are not connected to Google Photos.

The library still belongs to the fictional **Aarav Sharma** (born 1998, Delhi). People tags are pre-labelled. The app does not run face recognition.

Photos added with **Add** are copied from this phone onto the computer running the demo, only for that browser session.

Face groups are **simulated**: the same drawn characters (Papa, Mummy, Diya, Aarav, Karan, Neha, Rohit) are reused so a study participant can recognise who is in a frame. The app does not run face recognition.

Documents, screenshots and health objects are generated cards with visible text so `text_in_image` matches what is drawn. No Unsplash or Pexels files are shipped; the planted tasks need exact visuals (a blue cake, a blue door, a solo Papa) that stock photos would not guarantee.

Do not replace these with real personal photos.
