# Critical-incident interview guide (Part 3)

**Do not use a Google Form as the interview.** 30–40 minutes, 5–6 people.

## Screener (must pass)

- Google Photos is their library of record (not only WhatsApp)
- Library spans multiple years / feels “too big to scroll”
- In the last 12 months they searched a **life topic** (college, a trip, a certificate) and the grid mixed photos with documents or screenshots — or they could not tell which tile was the kind they meant

## Protocol

1. “Walk me through the last time you searched for a topic and the results felt like the wrong *kind* of item — or you could not tell.”
2. Exact words they remembered at the start (write verbatim, later paraphrase for the deck).
3. What showed up besides what they wanted (certificates, screenshots, random camera photos).
4. What they typed or tapped (search, people, albums, scroll).
5. How they knew they had the *right kind* of item if they found it.
6. Workaround if they failed (scroll, Files, WhatsApp, ask someone, give up).
7. Optional: watch one live topic search on their own library. Note type mix, not just “miss.”

## Do not chase (scoped out)

- Rahul-class: People tab / one relative only / deceased parent.
- Akshay-class: delete unused photos, storage cleanup.

## Ethics

First names only on notes; no names on the deck. Notes view-only for graders. No personal photos in the MVP.
