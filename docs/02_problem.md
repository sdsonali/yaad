# Problem lock

**Segment:** People who search Google Photos with a **coarse life-topic memory** (college, a trip, “that certificate”) across a large, mixed library.

**Scenario:** They type the topic. Photos returns **one undifferentiated grid** of camera photos, screenshots, and documents. The intended item may already be in the set, but they cannot evaluate it — or they lock onto the wrong kind (certificate instead of college photos).

**Product outcome:** Visual evaluation + result relevance by **media type**. Not a better first keyword. Not delete. Not face-ID.

**Root cause:** Topic search is type-blind. Memories, paperwork, and screenshots share the same topic words and the same grid.

**Workarounds:** Scroll past certificates to find faces; open Documents/Files; give up and ask someone.

**Value:** Topic search stays useful. Users can pick the *kind* they meant without reformulating the query.

**Hero evidence:** Ritu (college photos mixed with certificates); Swati (wants personal / documents / screenshots sequenced or separated).
